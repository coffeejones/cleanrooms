# Open-ended recurring agreements

The series editor offers 1, 3 and 6 month presets plus **Uden slutdato**. Weekday buttons show single-letter Danish labels with full accessible names; selected days are dark. Section numbers are removed. Duration selection and end-field visibility update immediately while scoped data loads; saving remains gated on the completed context.

An open-ended series is one versioned template with `openEnded: true`. Its indexed `endDate` uses the existing supported calendar maximum (`2100-12-31`); this is an internal range boundary, never a product expiry date or displayed count. Finite series retain actual dates. Closing an open template writes `openEnded: false`; earlier phases can contain several years of history. This avoids extending or materializing a new six-month series repeatedly.

`Series.enumerate` requires explicit date bounds for all open-ended or longer-than-366-day templates, and every occurrence query is limited to 366 days. Editor context, staffing and previews use a six-month working window, labeled as a preview. Calendar and point-detail reads retain their own requested dates, including future years. Summary counts use arithmetic; an endless agreement has no finite total, expiry or extension action. Manual exceptions are remapped by recurrence membership instead of full enumeration.

Editor save/delete reconciliation uses bounded working windows and affected exception dates. Template dates remain authoritative for the entire agreement. Truncating a series retains its earlier history, and undo preserves the explicit end mode. Publication lineage only expands known published/origin/exception weeks. Existing publication validation still checks overlap, approved absence, authorization and record generations within the selected publication period. Saving a draft retains the existing behavior: the editor previews conflicts in its working window; publication is the authoritative check for each published period.

Validation includes save/reload through the local browser, finite-to-open and open-to-finite actual editor transactions, history-preserving future splits, exact future-year point/calendar reads, bounded long-range expansion, compact summary flag preservation, old-client field-drop protection, stale versions, future publication overlap/absence rejection and transitive publication lineage.
